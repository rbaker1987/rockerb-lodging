Memorial Day Tournament – Lodging & Cost Guide

Bottom line

Staying in a Mineral Wells hotel saves about $400–600 per family per weekend compared with Rocker B's onsite rooms. A shared lake house only beats a hotel on price when four families split one 5–6 bedroom house. Its real advantages are the 10-minute drive, a kitchen and team time.

Estimated cost per family (4-day weekend, 3 nights)

A Mineral Wells hotel is the cheapest option for every family size. These are estimates, not quotes.

  • Food: packing most meals and eating out 3–4 times: $150–270 for 2 people, $315–580 for 5.
  • Local driving: hotel to ranch is about 17 miles each way, $20–40 for the weekend; about $10 from a lake house.
  • Not included: gas from home (about $14–15 per 100 miles at 25 mpg), extras like Rocker B gear or photos ($50–250), and a Thursday night if your first game is early Friday (about $160–180 per hotel room).
  • Team entry fee: covered by team fees.

A bigger family in a shared house may need its own bedroom or two, so splits may not be equal.

Mineral Wells hotels

The well-rated hotels run about $158–180 a night with taxes, about 16–17 miles from the ranch. Prices are for 1 room, 2 adults + 2 kids, Fri–Sun (2 nights).

Cheaper rooms (under $120/night) exist but are rated 5.4–6.2 out of 10. The Days Inn has a microwave and fridge in every room and free breakfast. Most listings offered free cancellation.

Lake houses near Graford

A house that sleeps 12 near Possum Kingdom Lake runs about $1,500–2,250 for a Fri–Sun weekend. Totals are Airbnb's 2-night prices for 12 guests.

Memorial Day warning: on VRBO, many houses near Rocker B require 3 nights or block Friday check-in that weekend. Plan on Fri–Mon for a house, and book early.

Rocker B basics

Rocker B Ranch is at 1125 Chaney Lane, Graford, TX 76449. You don't have to stay onsite to play: the minimum stay only applies to families who book a ranch room.

Before you book

Book only refundable rooms until Rocker B confirms our 12U dates on January 1.

  • Room occupancy: many 2-queen hotel rooms officially cap at 4 people. Families of 5 should call about a rollaway or suite.
  • Ask about a fridge and microwave: with a cooler for the fields, packing food saves the most money.
  • Carpool to the gate: the $25/day gate fee is per car, so families sharing a house or hotel can ride together.
  • Trailers and vans pay $50/day at the gate.
  • Lake houses for Memorial Day: expect a 3-night minimum (Fri–Mon). They go fast.
  • Groceries: Graford has few stores, so shop in Mineral Wells or Graham before heading out.

Interested in sharing a house?

Let the team know in the group chat: how many people are going, whether you prefer a house or a hotel, whether you need your own bedroom, if you're open to Fri–Mon, and how many cars you'll bring. Four families in one house is where a house makes sense on price.
